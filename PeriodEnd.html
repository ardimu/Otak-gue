Period-end tools

Everything here creates draft journals. Review them in Draft Management, then post them.

Used by depreciation, reversing entries, and year-end closing.

Depreciation

Straight-line, from the Fixed Assets sheet. Months already booked are not booked again, and a missed month is caught up.

Reversing entries

Choose posted adjusting entries of the month to reverse (usually accruals). The reversal is dated at the start of the next month.

Year-end closing

Choose the last month of the financial year. Profit and loss accounts are closed to Income Summary, then to Retained Earnings. Owner withdrawals and dividends are closed too.

Lock date

Journals dated on or before this date cannot be posted.
